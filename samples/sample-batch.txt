City Buses to Run Every Ten Minutes on Main Routes

The city transport office said on Monday that buses on the three busiest routes will run every ten minutes during the day from January, instead of every twenty minutes. The change affects routes 5, 12 and 21, which together carry more than half of all bus passengers in the city.

Officials said six new buses will be bought for the service at a cost of about 2.4 million dollars, paid partly by a government grant. Fares will not change. Evening and Sunday timetables will stay as they are for now, but the office said it will look at them after six months.

Passenger groups welcomed the plan, though some asked why smaller routes in the outer suburbs were left out. The transport office said those routes will be reviewed next year.

School Garden Project Wins Regional Award

A primary school's vegetable garden has won the regional environment award, the education department announced on Friday. Pupils aged 7 to 11 at Hillview Primary School grow beans, tomatoes and herbs on a plot that was once a car park, and they use the harvest in the school kitchen.

The judges praised the project for involving every class and for saving water by collecting rain from the roof. The school's headteacher, Priya Nair, said the garden began four years ago with ten parent volunteers and a small donation from a local shop.

The prize includes 5,000 dollars, which the school plans to spend on a greenhouse. A group of pupils will present the garden at a regional event next month.
