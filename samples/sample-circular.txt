Circular No. 7/2026: Revised Procedure for Travel Claims

Subject: Changes to Circular No. 3/2024 on travel claims

1. This circular modifies Circular No. 3/2024 dated 12 March 2024. Only the clauses listed as changed below are new. All other clauses of Circular No. 3/2024 continue to apply as they are.

2. Clause 2 (time limit) is changed. Travel claims must now be submitted within 15 days of the end of the journey. The earlier limit was 30 days. Claims submitted after 15 days will be returned unless the section head gives written approval.

3. Clause 3 (documents) is unchanged. Original tickets or boarding passes must be attached to every claim.

4. Clause 4 (approval) is changed. Claims above 20,000 rupees now need the approval of the finance officer in addition to the section head. Claims of 20,000 rupees or less need the section head only.

5. Clause 5 (advance payment) is unchanged. An advance of up to 50 percent of the estimated cost may be requested before the journey.

6. Clause 6 (new) is added. All claims must be submitted through the online portal. Paper claims will not be accepted from 1 December 2026.

7. These changes apply to all journeys that begin on or after 1 November 2026. Section heads must inform their staff and ensure that the new procedure is followed from that date. The finance office will give a short demonstration of the portal on 20 October 2026.

Issued by the Office of the Director of Administration.
